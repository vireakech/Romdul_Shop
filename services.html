Beyond the instrument — care, craft and guidance

Whether you're buying your first Roneat or restoring a family heirloom, our workshop in Phnom Penh supports the whole life of the instrument.

Tuning & Repair

Re-skinning drums, restringing lutes, re-tuning gongs and bars, and general upkeep to keep your instrument sounding as it should.

Custom Commissions

Choose your wood, tuning and decorative carving, and our artisans will build an instrument to your exact specification.

Traditional Music Lessons

One-on-one and small-group lessons in Tro, Roneat, Chapei and Khim, taught by working musicians in person or over video call.

Event Rental & Performance

Rent a full pinpeat ensemble with musicians for weddings, ceremonies and cultural events, delivered and set up on site.

Worldwide Shipping

Every instrument is padded, crated and insured for international transit, with tracked delivery to most countries.

Restoration

Careful restoration of antique and family instruments, matching original materials and finishing techniques where possible.
